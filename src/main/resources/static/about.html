About CineMax

Good films deserve a good seat.

CineMax is a cinema chain serving Negombo and Colombo. We show Sri Lankan, Tamil and international films on standard, premium recliner and VIP screens, with IMAX and 3D where the film calls for it.

  • Loyalty points on every paid seat, shown in your profile.
