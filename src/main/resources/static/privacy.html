Privacy

We only collect what we need to sell you tickets and look after your account.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number (optional) and a password, stored as a salted hash.
  • Booking details: the films, showtimes and seats you book, and your payment records. We store only the last four digits of a card.
  • Loyalty points: the points you earn from paid bookings.

How we use it

We use your information to take bookings and payments, send booking confirmations and reminders, issue tickets, and handle refunds.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with the payment provider needed to complete a payment, and with cinema staff when you show your ticket at the entrance.

Your choices

You can update your details in My profile, change your password, or delete your account. Accounts with booking history can't be deleted, so that payment records stay accurate.

Contact

Privacy questions go to support@cinemax.lk.
